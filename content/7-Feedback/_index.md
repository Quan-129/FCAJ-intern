> Below are my personal reflections and feedback regarding my experience participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. I hope this provides the organizing team with practical insights to continuously improve the program in the future.

### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment** 
The working environment at FCAJ is highly dynamic, tech-driven, and open. Team members are always ready to assist each other with debugging or discussing software architecture, even outside of official working hours. The comfortable workspace made it easy for me to focus on coding. However, I think the program could incorporate more short Tech-talks or team-bonding sessions to allow different groups to cross-network and learn about each other's tech stacks.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admins** 
The Mentors possess strong technical expertise and provide incredibly dedicated guidance. Instead of micromanaging, they guided me to read technical documentation independently, allowed me the freedom to propose solutions, and conducted thorough code reviews (especially regarding Clean Code principles). The Team Admins were also exceptionally prompt in granting resource access, ensuring a smooth workflow.

**3. Relevance to Academic Major** 
The assigned tasks aligned perfectly with my Computer Science major. Combining foundational university knowledge with practical technologies like Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI/ML) helped me not only solidify my theoretical understanding but also learn how to apply it to solve real-world data processing pipelines.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development** 
FCAJ has been an excellent stepping stone. I learned how to set up Cloud infrastructure, became familiar with source code management, professional team collaboration workflows, and standard technical writing. Most importantly, I developed Systems Thinking for problem-solving through the practical experiences shared by senior members.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit** 
The team culture is very positive, encouraging innovation and embracing a "fail-fast" mindset. All contributions, from suggesting minimalist UI/UX designs to environment configurations, were respected despite my intern status. Team spirit was most evident during project sprints, where everyone cross-reviewed and supported each other to meet deadlines.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns** 
The policy providing practical resources (AWS Cloud environments) and flexible working hours helped me easily balance my university studies with project deadlines. Being able to participate in specialized internal training sessions was a significant privilege.

### Other Questions

*   **What were you most satisfied with during your internship?**
    I was most satisfied with being granted the autonomy to configure my environment and directly participate in building and testing data pipelines, rather than being confined to repetitive administrative tasks.
*   **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**
    The program could provide a more detailed roadmap during the first week (Onboarding week), along with a prerequisite reading list of technical documentation, so new interns can get up to speed with specialized services more quickly.
*   **Would you recommend your friends to intern here? Why?**
    Absolutely. This is an ideal environment for IT students who want to step out of their comfort zones, gain hands-on experience with Cloud/AI infrastructure, and cultivate a professional programming mindset.

### Suggestions & Desires

*   **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**
    It would be great to host internal 1-2 day "Mini-Hackathons" where interns form teams to solve specific challenges. This would strongly stimulate creativity and the application of technology.
*   **Do you want to continue with this program in the future?**
    I am very eager to continue this journey and tackle more complex challenges related to system development and AI Model integration.
*   **Other feedback (free sharing):**
    I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the FCAJ organizing committee, the Mentors, and Admins for their patience, enthusiasm, and for creating such a meaningful internship journey.
